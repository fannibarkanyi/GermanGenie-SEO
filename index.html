Frequently asked questions

Do I need to know German to use GermanGenie?

No prior knowledge of German is needed to get started with GermanGenie. The app is designed to be accessible to absolutely everyone, regardless of their current level. Whether you're a complete beginner or already have some experience, GermanGenie meets you where you are. For beginners, the app does a great job at teaching the basics in a simple, intuitive way. And as you progress, GermanGenie grows with you, helping you practice more efficiently and reach higher levels faster.

Is GermanGenie really free?

Yes, GermanGenie is completely free — no hidden fees, no premium tier, no credit card needed. Every lesson, every feature, all of it is available to everyone at no cost. We believe language learning should be accessible to anyone, anywhere, and that starts with making it free.

Do I need a subscription to use GermanGenie?

No subscription needed — just download the app and start learning. GermanGenie is completely free, forever.

How is GermanGenie different from Duolingo?

Unlike Duolingo, GermanGenie is built around its own puzzle method — you construct sentences by fitting together actual puzzle pieces, making word order feel natural and intuitive rather than something you have to memorize. This hands-on approach trains your brain to think in German from the very beginning. GermanGenie also covers the things that trip learners up the most — word order, der/die/das, and vocabulary — each with their own gamified lesson type. It's a more focused, structured way to learn that goes beyond simple translation exercises.
